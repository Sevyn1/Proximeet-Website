Good to know

A few things you might wonder.

Have another question? Talk to our team.

Who is ProxiMeet for?+

ProxiMeet is for adults aged 18 and older who want to connect with people nearby through shared interests and meet in public spaces.

What is ProxiMeet?+

ProxiMeet is an app for meeting people near you who love what you love — and turning that into a real-life hello.

What kinds of interests can I add?+

Everyday passions — think music, books, photography, gaming, film, fitness, and more. You choose what represents you.

How do I find people nearby?+

ProxiMeet shows you people around you on a map-style view, organized by the interests you share. Product details and location permissions will be explained before launch.

Do I have to meet someone?+

No. Connections start with a request you choose to send or accept — and chat only opens once both of you are in. What happens next is always up to you.

When can I try it?+

ProxiMeet is in development. A launch date and supported cities have not been confirmed here. Join the waitlist for product and availability updates.
