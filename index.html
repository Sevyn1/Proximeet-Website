Do your part

Meet smart, every time

The app brings the tools. You bring the habits.

The prep

  • Add an SOS contact before you meet anyone.
  • Set up your duress PIN — if you’re ever forced to check in, it quietly alerts your SOS contacts.

The place

  • Meet somewhere public and well-lit, never an isolated spot after dark.
  • Stick to green zones — the map marks them as safer. Red means keep out.
  • Lock in an exact meeting point ahead of time — no spur-of-the-moment street meetups.

The meetup

  • Check in the moment your timer ends, so the people you trust never worry.
  • Call 911 if someone is in danger. Help only when it’s safe to.
  • Keep your next few meetups in the app — share personal details once trust is earned.

The ground rules

  • Report anything that feels off — the report button is one tap away.
  • Respect boundaries and accept “no” — kindness is the baseline.

Good to know

Questions about ProxiMeet

Have another question? Talk to our team.

What is ProxiMeet, and who is it for?+

ProxiMeet helps you meet someone new based on shared interests and proximity — then arrange to meet in person. It’s built for adults 18 and older.

What kinds of interests can I add?+

Everyday passions — think music, books, photography, gaming, film, fitness, and more. You choose what represents you.

How do I find people nearby?+

ProxiMeet uses a map-style view to help you discover people nearby with shared interests. From there, you can send a connection request.

Are the profiles real?+

Yes — every person is verified before they’re able to connect and meet with you.

How do I connect safely?+

Every meetup is timed: you set how long it lasts, check in with your passcode when it ends, and if you miss a check-in your SOS contacts get an alert. You can also share your live location with someone you trust during a meetup — and you always choose which requests to accept and who to block. More meetup tips live in our Safety & Choice section.

What privacy options do I have?+

ProxiMeet doesn’t track your location until you go online, and you never appear online unless you choose to. You can save hidden places — like home or the office — where ProxiMeet keeps you offline automatically, and closing the app logs you out so you’re never visible in the background.

When can I try it?+

Right now — ProxiMeet is live. Download it on the App Store or Google Play. If it isn’t available in your city yet, join the waitlist and you’ll hear the day it arrives.

Do I have to meet someone?+

No. Connections start with a request you choose to send or accept — and chat only opens once both of you are in. What happens next is always up to you.
